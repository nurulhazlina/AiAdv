Find & Fix the Mistakes Diagnose & Correct

Each card below contains a wrong statement about AI or Machine Learning. Read it carefully, spot the error, and type the corrected version.

Mistake 1 — Wrong idea about hallucination

❌ "An AI hallucination happens when the AI becomes confused and shuts down, refusing to answer a question because it does not know the answer."

What actually happens when an LLM 'hallucinates'? Why is it more dangerous than simply refusing to answer?

Hallucination is when the model generates a confident, fluent-sounding answer that is factually wrong or made up — not when it refuses to answer.Halusinasi berlaku apabila model menghasilkan jawapan yang kelihatan yakin dan lancar tetapi salah dari segi fakta — bukan apabila ia menolak untuk menjawab.

Mistake 2 — Wrong idea about AI bias

❌ "AI bias is caused by programmers deliberately coding prejudice into the system. If a programmer is fair-minded, the AI system will be unbiased."

Where does AI bias actually come from? Is it always intentional?

Bias typically comes from the training data reflecting historical inequalities, not from the programmer's intent. Even well-meaning developers can produce biased systems if the data is skewed.Berat sebelah biasanya datang daripada data latihan yang mencerminkan ketidaksamaan sejarah, bukan daripada niat pengaturcara. Pembangun yang berniat baik sekalipun boleh menghasilkan sistem berat sebelah jika data condong.

Mistake 3 — Wrong prompting claim

❌ "Zero-shot prompting is the most powerful technique because giving no examples forces the AI to use its full intelligence, whereas providing examples limits its thinking."

What is the actual difference between zero-shot and few-shot prompting in terms of performance?

Few-shot prompting generally improves performance on specific tasks by showing the model the expected format and style. Zero-shot is convenient but not always better.Few-shot prompting umumnya meningkatkan prestasi pada tugasan khusus dengan menunjukkan format dan gaya yang dijangkakan kepada model. Zero-shot adalah mudah tetapi tidak sentiasa lebih baik.

  • Purpose: What will the AI help students do specifically?

  • Bias risk: Could the chatbot treat students from different backgrounds unfairly? How would you check?

🧠 Concept Check 5 Questions · Pass = 4/5

Test your understanding. Select one answer per question, then submit.

1. Which of these best describes an AI 'hallucination'?

✅ Correct. The core idea of ML is learning from labelled examples instead of following hand-coded rules.

2. In supervised learning, every training example must include a:

✅ Correct. Supervised learning is named "supervised" because a human provides the correct label for every training example.

3. Which of these is an example of AI being used in everyday life?

✅ Correct. Music recommendations use ML — the model learns your preferences from your listening history.

4. What is a "feature" in machine learning?

✅ Correct. Features are the measurable inputs used to describe each example. The model uses features to make predictions.

5. A spam filter that marks emails as "spam" or "not spam" is an example of:

✅ Correct. A spam filter assigns each email to one of two categories — this is classification, and it learns from labelled examples.
