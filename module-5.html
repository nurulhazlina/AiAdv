5.1 — Identifying a Real-World Problem

⏱ ~12 min Not started
▼

What makes a problem right for AI?

Not every problem needs AI. The best candidates share three qualities:Tidak semua masalah memerlukan AI. Calon terbaik berkongsi tiga kualiti:

  • Learnable patterns exist in data — the input reliably predicts the output.
  • Too slow or costly for humans at scale — automation saves real time or money.
  • Labelled examples can be collected — you can teach the model with real cases.

The quality and quantity of your data determines the ceiling of your model's performance. Data preparation is often 70–80% of a real AI project.Kualiti dan kuantiti data anda menentukan siling prestasi model anda. Penyediaan data sering menjadi 70–80% daripada projek AI sebenar.

🏷 Labels

Each training example needs a label — the correct answer. In supervised learning, a human expert provides labels. Quality labels require clear criteria and consistency.Setiap contoh latihan memerlukan label — jawapan yang betul. Dalam pembelajaran terarah, pakar manusia memberikan label. Label berkualiti memerlukan kriteria yang jelas dan konsisten.

✂ Train / Val / Test split

Never test your model on the same data it trained on. Typical split: 70% training, 15% validation, 15% test. The test set is sacred — touch it only at the very end.Jangan sekali-kali uji model anda pada data yang sama yang ia dilatih. Pembahagian tipikal: 70% latihan, 15% pengesahan, 15% ujian. Set ujian adalah suci — sentuh hanya pada penghujungnya.

5.3 — Training an AI Model

⏱ ~15 min Not started
▼

The training loop

Training is an iterative process: the model makes a prediction → compares to the label (calculates loss) → adjusts its internal weights to reduce that loss → repeats. One complete pass through all training data is called an epoch.Latihan adalah proses berulang: model membuat ramalan → bandingkan dengan label (kira kehilangan) → laras pemberat dalaman untuk mengurangkan kehilangan → ulang. Satu laluan lengkap melalui semua data latihan dipanggil epoch.

📉 Loss

Loss measures how wrong the model is. Lower is better. Cross-entropy (classification), mean squared error (regression). Loss must fall each epoch.Kehilangan mengukur seberapa salah model. Lebih rendah lebih baik. Entropi silang (klasifikasi), ralat min kuasa dua (regresi). Kehilangan mesti turun setiap epoch.

⚡ Learning Rate

How large a step the model takes when updating weights. Too high → overshoots. Too low → stuck. Typical start: 0.001.Seberapa besar langkah yang diambil model semasa mengemas kini pemberat. Terlalu tinggi → terlampaui. Terlalu rendah → tersekat. Permulaan tipikal: 0.001.

Overfitting: The model memorises training data instead of learning general patterns. Training loss keeps falling, but validation loss rises — the model fails on new data.

5.4 — Testing and Improving Your Model

⏱ ~12 min Not started
▼

Accuracy isn't everything

A model that correctly labels 99% of emails as "not spam" — simply by ignoring spam entirely — achieves 99% accuracy but catches zero actual spam. That number looks great on paper while the model is completely useless in practice. To get an honest picture, we need richer metrics that measure where the model succeeds and fails: Model yang melabel 99% e-mel dengan betul sebagai "bukan spam" — semata-mata dengan mengabaikan spam sepenuhnya — mencapai ketepatan 99% tetapi tidak menangkap satu pun spam sebenar. Angka itu kelihatan bagus di atas kertas sedangkan model tersebut tidak berguna sama sekali dalam amalan. Untuk mendapat gambaran jujur, kita memerlukan metrik yang lebih kaya yang mengukur di mana model berjaya dan gagal:

  • Precision — of every item the model predicted as positive, how many were actually positive? High precision means few false alarms. e.g. "90% of flagged emails really were spam"
  • Recall — of every item that was actually positive, how many did the model catch? High recall means few missed cases. e.g. "we caught 85% of all spam that arrived"
  • F1 Score — the harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. It penalises models that sacrifice one for the other, giving a single balanced score that only stays high when both are high.

There is always a trade-off: tuning a model to catch every positive (high recall) usually means more false alarms (lower precision), and vice versa. The right balance depends on the cost of each type of mistake in your specific problem. Sentiasa ada pertukaran: menala model untuk menangkap setiap positif (daya ingat tinggi) biasanya bermakna lebih banyak penggera palsu (ketepatan rendah), dan sebaliknya. Imbangan yang betul bergantung pada kos setiap jenis kesilapan dalam masalah khusus anda.

🔧 How to improve

Low accuracy? → Collect more data or fix class imbalance. High train / low val accuracy? → Reduce overfitting with dropout or more data. Consistently wrong on one class? → Examine those examples carefully.Ketepatan rendah? → Kumpul lebih banyak data atau betulkan ketidakseimbangan kelas. Ketepatan latihan tinggi / pengesahan rendah? → Kurangkan lampau-padanan dengan dropout atau lebih banyak data. Sentiasa salah pada satu kelas? → Periksa contoh-contoh tersebut dengan teliti.

🔄 Iterative refinement

AI development is never linear. You collect → train → test → find failures → improve data or model → repeat. Each cycle gets you closer to a reliable system.Pembangunan AI tidak pernah linear. Anda kumpul → latih → uji → cari kegagalan → tambah baik data atau model → ulang. Setiap kitaran menghampirkan anda kepada sistem yang boleh dipercayai.

✓ The test set is sacred. Evaluate on your test set only once — at the very end. If you look at test results and tune based on them, you are now indirectly training on them, and your reported accuracy will be optimistic.

🎤 Tell your AI's story

A great AI presentation must answer three questions — in every audience's language:Pembentangan AI yang hebat mesti menjawab tiga soalan — dalam bahasa setiap penonton:

  • ❶What problem does it solve? — lead with the human need, not the technology.
  • ❷How does it work? — keep it visual; one good diagram beats a page of equations.
  • ❸Can we trust it? — be honest about accuracy, bias, and limitations.

Non-technical audiences care most about impact; technical reviewers focus on methodology and robustness. Use the simulations below to practise all three before your real presentation.Penonton bukan teknikal paling peduli tentang impak; pengulas teknikal memberi tumpuan kepada metodologi dan keteguhan. Gunakan simulasi di bawah untuk berlatih ketiga-tiga sebelum pembentangan sebenar anda.
