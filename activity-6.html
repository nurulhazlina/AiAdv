Find & Fix the Mistakes Diagnose & Correct

Each card below contains a wrong statement about AI or Machine Learning. Read it carefully, spot the error, and type the corrected version.

Mistake 1 — Wrong AI category

❌ "ChatGPT, DALL-E and GitHub Copilot are already examples of AGI — they can do many different things, so they must be Artificial General Intelligence."

Doing multiple things is not the same as AGI. What is the actual definition of AGI, and why do current AI tools not qualify?

AGI means an AI that can perform any intellectual task a human can — flexibly, with understanding and common sense. Current AI tools are powerful but each is trained for specific domains; they lack general understanding, common sense and the ability to flexibly transfer knowledge across completely different tasks.AGI bermaksud AI yang boleh melakukan sebarang tugasan intelektual yang boleh dilakukan manusia — secara fleksibel, dengan pemahaman dan akal sehat. Alat AI semasa berkuasa tetapi setiap satu dilatih untuk domain tertentu; mereka kekurangan pemahaman umum, akal sehat dan keupayaan untuk memindahkan pengetahuan secara fleksibel merentasi tugasan yang sama sekali berbeza.

Mistake 2 — Wrong claim about AI and jobs

❌ "AI will completely replace all human workers within 10 years — there will be no need for any human skills in the future economy."

History shows technology changes the nature of work. What new roles has AI already created? What human skills remain irreplaceable?

AI is already creating new jobs: prompt engineers, AI trainers, data labellers, AI ethics officers, and AI literacy educators. Skills like creative thinking, empathy, ethical judgment, and complex relationship-building remain uniquely human. Technology historically shifts work rather than eliminating it entirely.AI sudah mencipta pekerjaan baharu: jurutera prompt, jurulatih AI, pelabel data, pegawai etika AI, dan pendidik literasi AI. Kemahiran seperti pemikiran kreatif, empati, penghakiman etika, dan pembinaan hubungan kompleks kekal unik manusia. Teknologi secara sejarahnya mengalih kerja dan bukannya menghapuskannya sepenuhnya.

Mistake 3 — Wrong claim about explainability

❌ "AI explainability (XAI) is only important for scientists and AI developers — regular citizens do not need to understand why an AI made a decision."

If AI decides your loan application, university admission or job shortlist — why do YOU have a right to know the reason? What could go wrong if you don't?

When AI makes high-stakes decisions — approving loans, flagging people for extra security screening, or recommending medical treatment — the people affected have a right to understand and challenge those decisions. Without explainability, bias can go undetected and unchallenged, disproportionately harming already vulnerable groups.Apabila AI membuat keputusan berisiko tinggi — meluluskan pinjaman, menandakan orang untuk pemeriksaan keselamatan tambahan, atau mencadangkan rawatan perubatan — orang yang terjejas berhak memahami dan mencabar keputusan tersebut. Tanpa kebolehjelasan, bias boleh tidak dikesan dan tidak dicabar, menyebabkan mudarat tidak setara kepada kumpulan yang sudah terdedah.
