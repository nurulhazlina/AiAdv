Find & Fix the Mistakes Diagnose & Correct

Each card below contains a wrong statement about AI or Machine Learning. Read it carefully, spot the error, and type the corrected version.

Mistake 1 — Wrong testing practice

❌ "A student trained an AI model and tested it on the same data she used for training. She got 99% accuracy and declared the model ready for use."

Why is testing on training data like giving a student the exam the night before? What would a fair test look like?

Testing on training data is like giving a student the exam questions the night before — the model has already 'seen' the answers. A separate, unseen test set is needed for a fair evaluation.Menguji pada data latihan seperti memberikan pelajar soalan peperiksaan semalaman — model sudah 'melihat' jawapannya. Set ujian yang berasingan dan belum dilihat diperlukan untuk penilaian yang adil.

Mistake 2 — Wrong metric choice

❌ "For our cancer detection AI, accuracy of 95% is a great result. Since only 5% of scans show cancer, a model that always predicts 'no cancer' would score 95% and be ready for clinical use."

When one class is very rare, why is accuracy misleading? What metric would actually matter for catching all cancer cases?

When one class is rare (like cancer), accuracy is misleading. Recall (sensitivity) — catching all real cancer cases — matters far more than overall accuracy for life-critical systems.Apabila satu kelas jarang berlaku (seperti kanser), ketepatan adalah mengelirukan. Recall (sensitiviti) — mengesan semua kes kanser sebenar — jauh lebih penting daripada ketepatan keseluruhan untuk sistem yang kritikal.

Mistake 3 — Wrong project approach

❌ "The best way to start an AI project is to collect as much data as possible first, then figure out what problem to solve with it later."

Why must you define the problem before collecting data? What can go wrong if you collect data first?

Without a clear problem definition, you don't know what data to collect, what labels to use, or how to measure success. The problem must drive the data collection, not the other way around.Tanpa definisi masalah yang jelas, anda tidak tahu data apa yang perlu dikumpulkan, label apa yang perlu digunakan, atau cara mengukur kejayaan. Masalah mesti mendorong pengumpulan data, bukan sebaliknya.

Create From Scratch Design

This is your chance to apply everything creatively. Design your own AI solution idea.

🎨 Your Challenge — Full AI Project Proposal: Design your own AI project by walking through each stage of the AI development pipeline. Choose any real problem that matters to you — in your school, community or Malaysia.

  • Problem: What is the problem you are solving? (1 sentence, specific and measurable)
  • Data collection: What data would you collect? How would you label it?
  • Train / Test split: How would you divide your data? Why keep the test set hidden?
  • Evaluation: Which metric matters most — accuracy, precision or recall? Why?
  • Prototype / MVP: What would the simplest working version of your AI do? How would you test it with real users?
🎨 Cabaran Anda — Cadangan Projek AI Penuh: Reka bentuk projek AI anda sendiri dengan mengikuti setiap peringkat saluran pembangunan AI. Pilih mana-mana masalah sebenar yang bermakna bagi anda — di sekolah, komuniti atau Malaysia anda.
