1.1 — What Is AI?

Intelligent machines — myth vs. reality

⏱ 8 min ○
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of making computers perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence — recognising faces, understanding speech, or making decisions. AI is not magic, and computers are not truly "thinking." They are recognising patterns in data, extremely fast.Kecerdasan Buatan (AI) ialah bidang yang menjadikan komputer melaksanakan tugas yang biasanya memerlukan kecerdasan manusia — mengenali wajah, memahami percakapan, atau membuat keputusan. AI bukan sihir, dan komputer tidak benar-benar "berfikir." Mereka mengenali corak dalam data, dengan sangat pantas.

1.2 — Programming and Automation

Rules, thermostats, and the limits of if-then logic

⏱ 10 min ○
Traditional programming works by writing explicit rules. A programmer reads the problem, understands the logic, and translates it into code. The computer follows those rules exactly — it cannot adapt if the world changes.Pengaturcaraan tradisional berfungsi dengan menulis peraturan eksplisit. Pengaturcara membaca masalah, memahami logik, dan menerjemahkannya ke dalam kod. Komputer mengikut peraturan tersebut tepat-tepat — ia tidak boleh menyesuaikan diri jika dunia berubah.
🌡️ Try it: Rule-based temperature controller
Threshold (°C): 24°C Click room to add heat 🔥
Notice how the rule if temp > threshold → AC on causes oscillation. And when conditions change, the rule doesn't adapt — it still just checks the threshold. A human would notice and adjust. The rule cannot.
Key TakeawayRule-based programming is powerful for predictable, well-defined problems. When the environment is complex and changing, hand-written rules become fragile and impossible to maintain.Pengaturcaraan berasaskan peraturan berkuasa untuk masalah yang boleh diramal dan ditakrifkan dengan baik. Apabila persekitaran menjadi kompleks dan berubah, peraturan tulisan tangan menjadi rapuh dan mustahil untuk diselenggara.

1.3 — What Machine Learning Adds ⭐

Teaching a computer with examples instead of rules

⏱ 15 min ○
Machine learning flips the process: instead of writing rules, you show examples. The computer looks at enough labelled examples and figures out the pattern itself. Let's explore this — can a computer learn to tell an Ayam from an Itik?Pembelajaran mesin membalikkan proses: bukannya menulis peraturan, anda menunjukkan contoh. Komputer melihat cukup banyak contoh berlabel dan mencari coraknya sendiri. Mari terokai ini — bolehkah komputer belajar membezakan Ayam daripada Itik?

Key TakeawayMachine learning doesn't need hand-written rules. It finds patterns in labelled data. The more examples you provide, the better — and it can automatically use features a human might not think to include.Pembelajaran mesin tidak memerlukan peraturan tulisan tangan. Ia mencari corak dalam data berlabel. Lebih banyak contoh yang anda berikan, lebih baik — dan ia boleh menggunakan ciri-ciri yang mungkin tidak terfikir oleh manusia.

1.5 — AI vs. Human Intelligence

What computers do better — and what they still can't

⏱ 8 min ○
AI is not "smarter" than humans — it's differently capable. Understanding where each excels helps you decide when to trust an AI system, when to question it, and when human judgement is irreplaceable.AI tidak "lebih bijak" daripada manusia — ia mempunyai keupayaan yang berbeza. Memahami di mana setiap satu cemerlang membantu anda memutuskan bila untuk mempercayai sistem AI, bila untuk meraguinya, dan bila pertimbangan manusia tidak boleh digantikan.

🧠 Humans excel at…

💡 Common sense reasoning
🎨 True creativity & originality
🤝 Empathy & social nuance
🌍 Adapting to new contexts
⚖️ Moral & ethical judgement
📖 Learning from 1–2 examples

🤖 AI excels at…

⚡ Processing huge datasets fast
🔁 Consistent, tireless repetition
📊 Spotting patterns in noise
🌐 Scaling to millions of users
🔬 Precision in trained domains
💾 Never forgetting training data
Key TakeawayThe best outcomes happen when humans and AI work together — AI handles the scale and pattern recognition, humans handle the judgement, ethics, and creativity.Hasil terbaik berlaku apabila manusia dan AI bekerjasama — AI mengendalikan skala dan pengecaman corak, manusia mengendalikan pertimbangan, etika, dan kreativiti.
