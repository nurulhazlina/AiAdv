4.2.2 🔐 Understanding Large Language Models ▼

AI models you might already knowModel AI yang mungkin sudah anda kenali

✳ Claude ✦ Gemini ⊙ ChatGPT Grok 🐋 DeepSeek Qwen

These are all Large Language Models (LLMs) — AI designed to read, write, and understand human language. They were trained on billions of text documents and learned one fundamental skill.Ini semua adalah Model Bahasa Besar (LLM) — AI yang direka untuk membaca, menulis, dan memahami bahasa manusia. Mereka dilatih pada berbilion dokumen teks dan mempelajari satu kemahiran asas.

LLMs do one thing at their core: predict what comes next. Three key ideas to understand:LLM melakukan satu perkara pada terasnya: meramal apa yang datang seterusnya. Tiga idea utama untuk difahami:

📚 Trained on billions of text documents — books, articles, code, websites — to learn language patterns.
🔤 They never read whole words — text is broken into tokens (sub-word pieces). Each token becomes a number the model can process.

4.2.3 🧠 Inside the Transformer ▼

You type a message and press Enter. In under a second, your text is broken into tokens, each mapped to a vector of thousands of numbers, processed through dozens of stacked neural layers, and a response begins to stream back — one token at a time.Anda menaip mesej dan tekan Enter. Dalam masa kurang sesaat, teks anda dipecahkan kepada token, setiap satu dipetakan kepada vektor beribu nombor, diproses melalui berpuluh lapisan neural bertindih, dan respons mula mengalir balik — satu token pada satu masa.

Plain-English guide to the 6 pipeline steps 👇Panduan bahasa mudah untuk 6 langkah saluran paip 👇

Tokenise

Chop the text into small chunks called tokens — roughly one word or syllable each. "unhappy" → ["un","happy"]. The model only ever sees numbers, so each token gets an ID.Potong teks kepada cebisan kecil dipanggil token — kira-kira satu perkataan atau suku kata setiap satu. "unhappy" → ["un","happy"]. Model hanya melihat nombor, jadi setiap token mendapat ID.

Embed

Swap each token ID for a list of ~1000 numbers called a vector. Words with similar meanings get similar vectors — so "cat" and "kitten" end up close together in that number space.Tukar setiap ID token dengan senarai ~1000 nombor dipanggil vektor. Perkataan dengan maksud yang serupa mendapat vektor serupa — jadi "cat" dan "kitten" akhirnya berdekatan dalam ruang nombor itu.

Attend

Self-attention — every word looks at every other word and decides how much to "pay attention" to it. "it" in "The cat sat because it was tired" attends strongly to "cat".Perhatian-diri — setiap perkataan melihat setiap perkataan lain dan memutuskan berapa banyak untuk "memberi perhatian" kepadanya. "it" dalam "The cat sat because it was tired" memberi perhatian kuat kepada "cat".

Layer×N

The attention + maths is repeated through many layers stacked on top of each other (32–96 in big models). Each layer refines the meaning a little deeper — early layers catch grammar, deep layers catch complex reasoning.Perhatian + matematik diulang melalui banyak lapisan yang bertindih antara satu sama lain (32–96 dalam model besar). Setiap lapisan memperhalusi makna sedikit lebih dalam — lapisan awal menangkap tatabahasa, lapisan dalam menangkap penaakulan kompleks.

Predict

After all the layers, the model scores every word in its vocabulary (~50 000 words) for "what comes next?" — giving a probability distribution. Temperature controls how spread-out those probabilities are.Selepas semua lapisan, model memberi skor setiap perkataan dalam perbendaharaan katanya (~50 000 perkataan) untuk "apa yang datang seterusnya?" — memberi taburan kebarangkalian. Suhu mengawal seberapa tersebar kebarangkalian tersebut.

Generate

One token is picked from those probabilities, appended to the sequence, and the whole pipeline repeats — token by token — until the answer is complete. That is why AI "thinks" word by word, not all at once.Satu token dipilih daripada kebarangkalian tersebut, ditambahkan kepada urutan, dan seluruh saluran paip diulang — token demi token — sehingga jawapan lengkap. Itulah sebab AI "berfikir" perkataan demi perkataan, bukan sekaligus.

Most leading LLMs — GPT, Llama, Gemini — share the same core design: the decoder-only Transformer.Kebanyakan LLM terkemuka — GPT, Llama, Gemini — berkongsi reka bentuk teras yang sama: Transformer hanya-penyahkod.

➡️ The decoder reads tokens left-to-right, building its response one word at a time.
🔒 Masked self-attention — each token can only look at previous tokens, never forward. This is what makes autoregressive generation possible.

🎯 Prompting techniques: Zero-shot vs Few-shot

How many examples you provide changes how the AI interprets your task:Berapa banyak contoh yang anda berikan mengubah cara AI mentafsir tugas anda:

● Zero-shot

No examples given. The AI uses its pre-trained knowledge to attempt the task directly. Works well for common tasks.Tiada contoh diberikan. AI menggunakan pengetahuan pra-latihnya untuk mencuba tugas secara langsung. Berfungsi baik untuk tugas biasa.

Q: Classify this review as Positive or Negative:
“The food was cold and service was slow.”

A: Negative

✓ Simple, fast. Best for clear, everyday tasks the model was trained on.✓ Mudah, pantas. Terbaik untuk tugas jelas dan harian yang dilatih model.

⚙ Few-shot

Provide 2–5 labelled examples first. The AI learns the pattern from your examples, then applies it.Berikan 2–5 contoh berlabel dahulu. AI mempelajari corak daripada contoh anda, kemudian menggunakannya.

“Excellent staff!” → Positive
“Never coming back.” → Negative
“Decent but pricey.” → ?

✓ More accurate for specialized, ambiguous, or domain-specific tasks.✓ Lebih tepat untuk tugas khusus, samar-samar, atau spesifik-domain.

⚠️ Hallucinations: when AI makes things up

LLMs generate plausible-sounding text, not verified facts. When uncertain, they hallucinate — confidently stating things that are false.LLM menjana teks yang kedengaran munasabah, bukan fakta yang disahkan. Apabila tidak pasti, mereka berhalusinasi — menyatakan perkara yang salah dengan yakin.

⚠️ Why it happens: LLMs are optimised for fluency, not truth. They have no built-in fact-checker — they just predict what word comes next. Always verify AI outputs on important topics.⚠️ Mengapa ia berlaku: LLM dioptimumkan untuk kefasihan, bukan kebenaran. Mereka tiada pemeriksa fakta terbina dalam — mereka hanya meramal perkataan seterusnya. Sentiasa sahkan output AI pada topik penting.

🎯 Key limitations to understand

❓
AI makes mistakes
When it cannot find an answer, it guesses what you want to hear — and it does so confidently. Always cross-check important outputs with reliable sources.
🧮
A very good “copy-cat”
AI learns from the internet. If something incorrect was repeated often enough online, the model may treat it as truth. It mirrors the quality — and biases — of its training data.
🤖
Not a real brain
AI has no real feelings, common sense, or moral judgment. It cannot tell right from wrong on its own — it only predicts what text seems most likely to follow.
💡
No true creativity
AI cannot replicate human imagination, intuition, or genuine original thought. It recombines patterns from training data — sophisticated pattern-matching, not true invention.

✅ Responsible AI use: Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do's

✓Review AI's output by cross-checking with verified sources
✓If you doubt the output, refine your prompt first — your instinct may be right
✓Be clear, specific, and concise in your prompts
✓Break big, complex tasks into smaller, focused parts
✓Leave a positive digital footprint — use AI ethically and responsibly

❌ Don'ts

✗Share personal info, private ideas or confidential data with AI
✗Fully trust everything AI tells you — it can sound very convincing even when wrong
✗Let AI replace your own thinking, creativity, and common sense
✗Use AI to do your homework or assignments without understanding the work
✗Ignore the real costs — AI training and inference consumes enormous energy

🌟 Remember: We are all responsible for making AI safer. AI is a powerful tool — and like all powerful tools, the results depend entirely on how thoughtfully you use it.🌟 Ingat: Kita semua bertanggungjawab menjadikan AI lebih selamat. AI adalah alat yang berkuasa — dan seperti semua alat berkuasa, hasilnya bergantung sepenuhnya pada seberapa bijaksana anda menggunakannya.

4.3.3 🔬 Feature Extraction Pipeline ▼

Raw sensor readings are noisy — they need cleaning before an AI model can use them.Bacaan penderia mentah adalah bising — ia memerlukan pembersihan sebelum model AI boleh menggunakannya.

① Raw readings are sampled and normalised — removing spikes and scaling values to a standard range.
② Key statistics (mean, variance, frequency peaks) are extracted as features the model can work with.
③ The cleaned values are packed into a feature vector — a compact list of numbers fed into the AI model.

When AI is unfair

AI models learn from historical data. If that data reflects existing inequalities, the model amplifies them. Detecting and mitigating bias is a core engineering responsibility.Model AI belajar daripada data sejarah. Jika data tersebut mencerminkan ketidaksamaan sedia ada, model mengamplifikasikannya. Mengesan dan mengurangkan kecenderungan adalah tanggungjawab kejuruteraan teras.
